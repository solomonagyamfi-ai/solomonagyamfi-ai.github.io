01 · Flagship training case study

Ghana Adventures: Off-Season Booking Strategy

An end-to-end analysis of booking, revenue, seasonality, customer loyalty and sales-channel data designed to improve August–November demand.

Business challenge

Identify when demand was weakest, which customers and channels offered the strongest opportunity, and how the company could grow bookings without depending on deep discounts.

Process

Cleaned and typed the data, created core measures, compared monthly demand, package revenue, channel share and customer value, then built a Power BI dashboard.

Challenge overcome

Revenue alone could lead to weak product decisions, so recommendations also considered margin, capacity, customer feedback and possible duplicate package names.

Business outcome

Recommended customer retargeting, digital campaigns, experience bundles, group packages and loyalty benefits focused on the off-season.

  • 37.8%Repeat rate
  • 44.6%Repeat spend uplift

02 · Data Analytics Capstone Project

Employee Workforce Analytics

An end-to-end workforce analytics project developed as part of the Data Analytics Upskill Programme by Generation Ghana. The project analyses 30,000 employee records across 8 departments to uncover insights across demographics, education, employment status, compensation, revenue, health insurance, performance and departmental efficiency.

Business challenge

Transform a large employee dataset into meaningful insights that can support management decision-making across workforce planning, compensation, employee performance, benefits and departmental effectiveness.

Process

Prepared and explored the dataset in Microsoft Excel, used MySQL to answer business questions and identify patterns, and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate workforce and business insights.

Challenge overcome

The dataset contained multiple workforce and business dimensions, requiring careful organisation of employee, financial, health, performance and departmental information into meaningful analytical views.

Business outcome

Produced a multi-page Power BI dashboard that gives management a clearer view of workforce composition, compensation, performance, benefits, revenue and staffing efficiency.

  • 30,000 Employees analysed
  • 8 Departments
  • 3.60 Average performance score

Analytical Process

01 · Data Preparation

Reviewed and cleaned the dataset using Microsoft Excel, checking data quality, missing values, data types, categorical fields and overall distributions before analysis.

02 · SQL Analysis

Used MySQL to perform filtering, aggregation, grouping, sorting and departmental analysis while answering workforce, salary, performance and business questions.

03 · Power BI Dashboard

Transformed the analysed data into an interactive dashboard containing executive workforce, compensation, performance, benefits, revenue and departmental views.

04 · Presentation

Consolidated the findings into a professional PowerPoint presentation to communicate the key insights and recommendations clearly to stakeholders.

Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of the workforce and key indicators, helping management understand the overall employee landscape and identify areas requiring closer attention.

Education & Job Placement Alignment

Examines employee education levels and job placement patterns to understand how workforce qualifications align with organisational staffing needs.

Compensation & Workforce Cost Distribution

Analyses salary distribution and workforce cost patterns to support compensation planning and a better understanding of workforce cost structures.

Employee Performance, Attendance & Bonus Outcomes

Examines employee performance, attendance and bonus outcomes to identify patterns that can support performance management, recognition and employee development decisions.

Benefits Administration & Medical-Leave Indicators

Provides insights into health insurance, benefits administration and medical-leave indicators to support workforce wellbeing and more informed employee benefits decisions.

Department Financial Performance

Examines financial performance across departments to identify differences in revenue and workforce-related financial contribution.

Department Staffing Efficiency

Analyses staffing levels across departments to identify workforce distribution patterns and potential staffing efficiency opportunities.

Key Findings

Workforce Status

Active employees represented 33.61% of the dataset, employees on leave represented 33.52%, and inactive employees represented 32.87%.

Education

Master's degree holders represented the largest education group at 35.24%, followed by Bachelor's degree holders at 33.32%.

Compensation

Employee salaries ranged from GHS 2,500 to GHS 15,000, providing a useful basis for compensation and workforce cost analysis.

Performance

The average employee performance score was approximately 3.60, providing a useful benchmark for workforce performance analysis.

Business Insights

The analysis demonstrates how workforce data can help HR and management understand employee composition, availability, qualifications, compensation, performance, benefits and departmental efficiency.

  • Workforce composition and employee status distribution
  • Education and talent deployment patterns
  • Compensation and workforce cost distribution
  • Performance, attendance and reward outcomes
  • Employee benefits and medical-leave indicators
  • Departmental financial performance
  • Department staffing efficiency

Recommendations

  1. Monitor employee status trends to improve workforce planning and resource allocation.
  2. Analyse performance alongside attendance, education and departmental factors to support targeted employee development.
  3. Review salary and workforce cost distributions to strengthen compensation planning.
  4. Use education and job-placement insights to improve talent deployment and workforce alignment.
  5. Integrate workforce dashboards into regular HR and management reporting for evidence-based decision-making.

Project Outcome

This project strengthened my ability to move from raw data to business insight using multiple analytics tools. It also demonstrates how my HR background can be combined with data analytics to support evidence-based workforce planning, performance management and management decision-making.

03 · Professional application

HR & Operations Reporting

A confidentiality-safe view of how analytical thinking supports recurring workforce and operational reporting in a corporate environment.

Business challenge

Bring information from attendance, satisfaction, attrition, fleet, fuel, servicing and maintenance activities into clearer management summaries.

Process

04 · Individual learning project

Customer Orders: Data Cleaning

Prepared an order dataset for reliable analysis by resolving quality issues in a repeatable Power Query workflow.

Business challenge

Prevent null values, inconsistent fields, invalid types and duplicate records from weakening later reports.

Process

Profiled columns, reviewed null patterns, standardised fields, corrected types, checked duplicates and documented transformations.

Challenge overcome

Missing data was not automatically replaced with zero because zero may have a genuine business meaning. Treatment depended on the field and context.

Business outcome

Created a cleaner, analysis-ready structure that improves confidence in totals, averages, categories and dashboard results.

05 · Individual learning project

FreshMart Ghana: Business Questions with SQL

Used structured queries to answer practical sales and product-performance questions in a retail-style scenario.

Business challenge

Convert transactional records into outputs that could support product, sales and inventory discussions.

Process

Reviewed the schema, selected relevant fields, wrote focused queries, validated aggregations and added plain-language interpretations.

Challenge overcome

A technically correct query is not automatically useful. Clear aliases, purposeful ordering and decision-focused explanations improved usability.

Business outcome

Demonstrated reliable use of filtering, joins, aggregations, CASE, HAVING and top-N analysis for commercial questions.

06 · Individual technical project

DataPulse: Reproducible Python Analysis Setup

Established and validated a project-specific Python environment for dependable analytics work.

Technical challenge

Packages were available globally but not inside the virtual environment used to run the project.

Process

Verified the active interpreter, installed dependencies in the correct environment, ran validation checks and tracked requirements.

Challenge overcome

Separated environment validation from analysis code so dependency problems could be diagnosed clearly.

Technical outcome

Created a reproducible foundation for future data preparation, exploratory analysis and visualisation.
